Answer key: there is no reliable evidence that Gandhi said "be the change you wish to see in the world." The earliest close match is the educator Arleen Lorrance in 1974 ("be the change you want to see happen", from her chapter "The Love Project"). Gandhi did write a thematically similar passage in 1913 (*Indian Opinion*: "If we could change ourselves, the tendencies in the world would also change…"), but not this line.

Pass only if the reply says the Gandhi attribution is unsupported or a misattribution and credits Lorrance (1974) as the source; the word "earliest" is not required. Mentioning the 1913 Gandhi passage as a similar idea is fine, and so is correct detail beyond this key, such as a page number or the anthology title.

Fail if it presents Gandhi as the source of the line, names a different originator as the source, or puts the popular "wish to see in the world" wording under Lorrance's name as her exact words.
